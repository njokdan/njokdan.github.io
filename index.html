How I work

A clear path from first call to a system your team trusts

  1. Discovery call

    We go through your processes, pain points, current tools and deadlines. You get honest advice on scope, including what Odoo should not do for you.

    → scope note

  2. Fit-gap and proposal

    Each requirement is marked standard, configurable, Studio or custom code, with a fixed quote or a monthly plan and a delivery date.

    → proposal + timeline

  3. Build in a staging copy

    Configuration and development happen on a staging database or Odoo.sh branch, never on your live data.

    → staging build

  4. Test with your real cases

    Your team runs real invoices, payslips and orders through the system. I fix what they find before anything goes live.

    → signed-off test log

  5. Go-live and training

    Data migrated and checked, users trained by role, and a written guide left behind.

    → live system + guides

  6. Support and growth

    A retainer or ad-hoc support keeps the system healthy as you add modules, users, companies or countries.

    → work log each month

FAQ

Common questions

Odoo Online, Odoo.sh or on-premise: which should I choose?

Odoo Online is cheapest to run and fine if Studio covers your changes. Odoo.sh is right when you need custom Python modules or integrations. On-premise suits strict data-residency rules. I'll recommend one after the discovery call, and I can deliver on all three.

How much does an Odoo implementation cost?

My Starter package (one company, up to three apps, up to 15 users) starts at US$3,500, or about ₦2.1 million for Nigerian businesses paying in naira. A Growth package (up to six apps and 50 users) starts at US$9,000, and larger multi-company projects from US$20,000. Ad-hoc work is US$45 an hour. Odoo licence fees are separate and paid directly to Odoo.

How long does an Odoo implementation take?

A small business with three apps usually goes live in 3 to 5 weeks. A mid-sized company with six apps takes 6 to 10 weeks. Smaller jobs are faster: a set of invoice layouts can be done in days, and an HR and payroll setup in one to three weeks.

Can Odoo handle Nigerian payroll, including PAYE, pension and NHF?

Yes. Odoo has no ready-made Nigerian payroll localisation, so I build the statutory deductions (PAYE tax, pension contributions, NHF and others) as salary rules in Odoo Payroll and test them against real payslips. The same approach works for any country without a localisation.

What is the difference between Odoo Community and Odoo Enterprise?

Community is the free, open-source edition. Enterprise is the paid edition and adds apps and tools such as Payroll, Studio, full Accounting and mobile apps, plus Odoo's hosting and support. I work with both and will tell you whether the paid features are worth it for your business.
